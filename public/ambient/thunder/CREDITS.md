# Thunder

Short stretches cut from three recordings, all released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication), and saved again as Opus in WebM. Nothing else
was changed: Groovium filters and evens them out while playing them.

- `thunder-a.webm`: 18.5 s to 38 s of "Rain + Long Thunder" by WuxiaScrub,
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-long-thunder
- `thunder-b.webm`: `sfx100v2_thunder_01.ogg` from "100 CC0 SFX #2" by
  rubberduck, https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2
- `thunder-c1.webm` to `thunder-c8.webm`: ten-second stretches of
  "Dark_Rainy_Night(ambience).ogg" from "rain and thunders" by kindland,
  starting at 0:32.5, 0:51.5, 1:28.5, 1:47, 2:24.5, 2:43, 3:20 and 4:16,
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-and-thunders
